## What it covers

- Minimum corridor and door widths along the routes you have in mind
- Lift brand, controller type and whether it has a service mode
- Floor surfaces, thresholds and ramps
- Wifi coverage on each route
- Candidate dock locations with a nearby socket
- The people who will operate the robot day to day
