We visit your site before anything is quoted. We measure corridor and door widths, check lift access and controller type, walk the floor surfaces and thresholds, test wifi coverage on the likely routes, and talk to the people who will use the robot every day. You get a written feasibility assessment. If the answer is that a robot will not work in your building, we say so.
