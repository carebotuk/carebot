## The problem

Floor staff are pulled between the till, the shelf and the customer asking where the toilets are. Promotional screens are static and stuck in one place.

## How robotics helps

KettyBot Pro greets at the entrance, carries the offer of the day on its front screen, and escorts a customer to the right aisle when asked. Between times it moves samples and light stock. The advertising screen is the first return on the unit; the walking is the second.

## What deployment looks like in a store

- **Aisle widths and floor surfaces** are measured before we quote.
- **Routes and no-go zones** are configured with your store manager, including tills and fitting rooms.
- **Content** is loaded and your team trained to update it.
