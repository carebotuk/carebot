## Give the robot a clear job on your shop floor

A service robot can greet visitors, display an offer or guide shoppers along configured routes. The useful question is where that would help your customers and staff in your store.

KettyBot Pro combines a front screen with open trays. You can use it for greetings and promotions, visitor guidance, or carrying suitable samples and light items between agreed points.

## Meet KettyBot Pro

Your team can update the display through the manufacturer’s software. We help configure the robot’s routes and show your staff how to manage its content and day-to-day operation.

It works alongside your people: staff still answer questions, advise customers and handle items at the destination. We will assess the proposed job and loads with you.

## Designed into your store layout

We check aisle widths, floor surfaces and turning space, including how these change around displays and busy areas. Routes, charging space and areas to avoid are agreed with your store manager.
