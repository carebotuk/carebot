## The problem

Night cover is where hotel labour cost bites hardest. One or two people on the desk, and every guest request for towels, toothpaste or a late meal pulls one of them away from reception for ten minutes at a time. Add a lift wait and a wrong floor, and out-of-hours service is slow, expensive, or both.

## How robotics helps

A delivery robot takes the walk. The desk loads the compartment, selects the room, and FlashBot Max calls the lift, navigates to the door and alerts the guest. The compartment opens only for them. The porter never leaves the desk.

During the day the same unit runs linen and minibar stock between floors, and a KettyBot Pro at reception greets arrivals and escorts them to the lift.

## What deployment looks like in a hotel

- **Lift integration** is the first question. Most major brands work without modification; we confirm at the survey.
- **Guest-facing behaviour.** Speed, yielding and how the robot announces itself are all configured, and we agree them with your front-of-house lead.
- **Corridor and door widths.** We measure every route, including fire doors, before we quote.
- **Staff training.** The desk team is trained to load, dispatch and recover the robot in under an hour.

## What we will not claim

We will not quote an hours-saved figure without a deployment behind it. We will put the robot in front of you at our Kent facility and run it through your scenario.
