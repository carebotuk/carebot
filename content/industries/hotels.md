## A delivery should not leave the desk short-handed

A guest needs fresh towels just as another arrives to check in. A colleague needs supplies on another floor. Small deliveries can create difficult choices for a busy hotel team.

A delivery robot can handle the journey on an agreed route. Staff prepare the items and arrange the handover; the robot carries them to the destination.

## Choose the robot for the job

**FlashBot Max** carries items in enclosed compartments. It is the model to consider for guest amenities and supply runs between floors, subject to lift compatibility and route assessment.

**KettyBot Pro** carries open trays and has a front display. Consider it for dining service, greetings and visitor guidance along configured routes.

## We check the whole delivery journey

For a room-delivery route, we assess the lift, fire doors, corridor clearances and floor surfaces, as well as the place where staff load the robot. Together, we agree how guests receive their items and how your team handles exceptions.

Installation includes route mapping and staff training. Purchase, lease and support terms are confirmed in your quotation.
